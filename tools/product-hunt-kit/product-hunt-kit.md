# Product Hunt Day-Of Playbook
An hour-by-hour plan for launch day plus the copy you'll need.

## 1. Two weeks before
- [ ] Create/refresh your maker profile; comment on 10 launches
- [ ] Tagline (60 chars max): outcome, not features
- [ ] Gallery: 4–6 images 1270×760, first one tells the whole story
- [ ] 60-second video (optional, helps)
- [ ] Schedule launch for 12:01 AM Pacific (3:01 AM Eastern)
- [ ] List of 50 supporters to message personally (no "upvote" asks — ask for honest feedback)

## 2. Maker first comment
> Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm [Name], maker of [Product]. I built it because [frustration story, 2 sentences]. What it does: [3 bullets]. What's next: [roadmap teaser]. Launch perk: [offer]. I'd love your honest feedback — what would make this a must-have for you?

## 3. Hour-by-hour (Eastern)
| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 3:01 AM | Launch live; post maker comment |
| 7:00 AM | Email list + X post + LinkedIn |
| 8–12 | Reply to every comment within 15 min |
| 12:00 PM | Midday update post with a screenshot or stat |
| 1–6 PM | Personal messages to supporters in other time zones |
| 6:00 PM | Thank-you post; share what you learned |
| 3:00 AM next | Day ends; screenshot final ranking |

## 4. Message templates
**Email:** "We're live on Product Hunt today — I'd love your honest take: [link]"
**DM:** "Hey [Name], launched [Product] today. You mentioned [pain] — curious if this helps. [link]"

## 5. Rules
- Don't ask for upvotes; don't buy them. It gets you penalised.
- Answer criticism with thanks and specifics.
- Launch day isn't the finish line — the follow-up email to signups is.

## 6. Post-mortem
Visitors · Signups · Conversion · Top referrer · Best comment · What to change next time
