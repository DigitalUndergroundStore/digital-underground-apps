# Pricing Page Kit
Price your product and write a pricing page people trust.

## Pick a model
- [ ] One-time price: tools people use occasionally (CA$9–49)
- [ ] Subscription: ongoing value, sync, or hosted cost (CA$3–15/mo)
- [ ] Free + paid: generous free tier, one clear upgrade reason
- [ ] Anchor: show a higher tier so the middle one feels right

## Price check
- [ ] List 3 alternatives and their prices
- [ ] Write the hours/money your product saves per month
- [ ] Price at 10–20% of the value saved
- [ ] End in 9 or a round number — pick one style everywhere

## Pricing page checklist
- [ ] Max 3 tiers, middle one marked "Most popular"
- [ ] Each tier: name, price, who it's for, 3–5 features
- [ ] Currency shown (CA$ / US$)
- [ ] Refund policy in one sentence
- [ ] FAQ: taxes, cancel anytime, devices, privacy
- [ ] One CTA per tier, verb-first ("Get Pro")

## Copy templates
Headline: Simple pricing for [audience]. No surprises.
Tier line: [Tier] — for [who] who want [outcome].
Guarantee: Not for you? Email within 14 days for a full refund.
