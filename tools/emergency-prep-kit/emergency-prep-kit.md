# Home Emergency Prep Kit
A 72-hour kit, a family plan and a contact sheet for power outages, storms and evacuations. Based on standard Get Prepared / Ready.gov guidance.

## 72-hour kit (per person unless noted)
- [ ] Water: 2 L per person per day for drinking + 2 L for washing (12 L total)
- [ ] Non-perishable food for 3 days + manual can opener
- [ ] Flashlight + extra batteries (or crank/solar)
- [ ] Battery or crank radio
- [ ] First aid kit; 7 days of prescription meds
- [ ] Copies of ID, insurance, prescriptions (paper + USB)
- [ ] Cash in small bills
- [ ] Phone chargers + power bank
- [ ] Whistle, multi-tool, duct tape
- [ ] Warm clothes, blankets, rain gear
- [ ] Hygiene: wipes, garbage bags, toilet paper
- [ ] Pet food, leash, carrier, vet records
- [ ] Baby items: formula, diapers
- [ ] Spare car and house keys

## Power outage
- Keep fridge/freezer shut (fridge ~4 h, full freezer ~48 h)
- Unplug electronics; leave one light on to know when power returns
- Never run generators or BBQs indoors
- Check on older neighbours

## Twice a year (when clocks change)
- [ ] Test smoke and CO alarms
- [ ] Rotate water and food
- [ ] Check meds and battery dates
- [ ] Update contact sheet
