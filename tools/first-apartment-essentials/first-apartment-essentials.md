# First Apartment Essentials
Everything you need for your first place, sorted by when you'll need it, with a budget sheet.

## 1. Before move-in
- [ ] Tenant insurance (often required by the lease)
- [ ] Set up hydro/electricity, internet and gas if not included
- [ ] Change your address with your bank, employer, government and mail forwarding
- [ ] Photograph every room on day one for the move-in inspection
- [ ] Locate the breaker panel, water shut-off and smoke detectors

## 2. Night-one box
- Toilet paper, hand soap, towel
- Phone charger, power bar
- Sheets, pillow, blanket
- Box cutter, scissors
- Snacks, water, paper plates
- Light bulbs, flashlight
- Basic meds

## 3. Kitchen
**Must have:** frying pan, medium pot, baking sheet, chef's knife, cutting board, spatula, wooden spoon, can opener, colander, measuring cups, 4 plates/bowls/mugs, cutlery, food storage containers, dish soap, sponge, dish towels.
**Later:** slow cooker or rice cooker, blender, Dutch oven, toaster.

## 4. Bathroom
Shower curtain and rings, bath mat, toilet brush, plunger, toilet paper, towels, garbage can, basic cleaner.

## 5. Bedroom
Mattress, sheets (2 sets), duvet, pillows, hangers, laundry basket, blackout curtains if needed.

## 6. Cleaning kit
All-purpose cleaner, glass cleaner, microfiber cloths, broom and dustpan, mop or Swiffer, vacuum (can wait), garbage and recycling bags, rubber gloves.

## 7. Tools
Screwdriver set, hammer, tape measure, picture hooks, level, Allen keys, duct tape, zip ties, stud finder (optional).

## 8. Safety
- [ ] Test smoke and CO alarms
- [ ] Fire extinguisher in the kitchen
- [ ] First-aid kit
- [ ] Emergency contacts on the fridge
