FAQ

Is it free to list?▼

Yes. Listing in the Developer Marketplace is free — list your products for free anytime. We do not take a cut of sales; buyers pay you on your own checkout.

How does review work?▼

Clean listings can go live after automated checks. Edge cases are held for a quick human review. We filter spam, scams, and malware; typical human review is a few days.

Do you handle payments?▼

No. This is link-out only. The Visit button sends people to your Gumroad, Stripe, App Store, or site with ?ref=digitalunderground when possible. Digital Underground does not process payouts for Developer Marketplace listings.

How do I edit my listing or see analytics?▼

Open your Creator dashboard with the private link shown after you submit. You can edit, unpublish, or delete listings and see views, clicks, and click-through rate.

Lost your dashboard link?▼

Lost your link? Email digitalunderground.store@proton.me from the address you used on your listing.
