# Moving Day Checklist
Move without the chaos.

## 8 weeks out
- [ ] Book movers or truck (get 3 quotes)
- [ ] Declutter: sell, donate, toss
- [ ] Start a moving folder: quotes, lease, receipts

## 4 weeks out
- [ ] Change address: Canada Post mail forwarding, CRA, bank, employer
- [ ] Update driver's licence and health card (provincial rules vary)
- [ ] Book elevator / parking permits
- [ ] Start packing rarely used rooms

## 1 week out
- [ ] Transfer hydro, gas, internet, insurance to new address
- [ ] Pack an "open first" box: chargers, meds, sheets, toilet paper, kettle
- [ ] Confirm movers' time and payment

## Moving day
- [ ] Photo meter readings at both homes
- [ ] Final walk-through: closets, cupboards, balcony
- [ ] Keep valuables and documents with you

## Box labels
Room · Contents · Priority (1 = open first) · Box # of total
