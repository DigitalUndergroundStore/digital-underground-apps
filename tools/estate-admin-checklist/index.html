Estate Admin Checklist

A calm, step-by-step guide for the executor or family after someone dies: first days, notifications, accounts, and settling the estate. General guidance for Canada and the US, not legal advice.

First 48 hours

  • [ ] Get the medical certificate / pronouncement of death
  • [ ] Contact a funeral home; they often register the death
  • [ ] Secure the home, car, pets, mail
  • [ ] Find the will and any funeral wishes
  • [ ] Tell close family and friends; ask one person to help with calls

First 2 weeks

  • [ ] Order 5–10 certified copies of the death certificate
  • [ ] Arrange the funeral or memorial
  • [ ] Locate key documents (see list below)
  • [ ] Contact the lawyer or notary who holds the will
  • [ ] Notify employer / pension administrator

Documents to find

Document Found? Where
Will (original)
Birth & marriage certificates
SIN / SSN
Bank and investment statements
Property deeds, mortgage
Insurance policies
Tax returns (last 2 years)
Vehicle ownership
Digital accounts list / password manager
