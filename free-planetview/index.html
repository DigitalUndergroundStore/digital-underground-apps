PlanetView Live

3D Earth, solar system & flight sim · In live beta (testing) · Free for a limited time with email signup

A photoreal Earth and solar system in your browser, plus a ship you fly from the launch pad. Join the Digital Underground email list and get PlanetView Live free for a limited time (normally $24). PlanetView is in live beta (testing), so expect regular updates and the occasional rough edge.

Beta testers: found a bug? Tell us — inside PlanetView Live tap the 🐞 Report a bug button (bottom-left), or use this form. Every report is read.

How you get it

  1. Sign up above (tick the consent box).
  2. Click the Gumroad link shown after signup. The code brings the price to $0; enter your email and Gumroad emails your license key.
  3. Open PlanetView, paste the key, and add it to your home screen if you like.

What you get

  • Photoreal Earth with live sunlight and the day/night terminator
  • The Sun through Pluto, plus Jupiter's moons
  • Manual flight, AI launch to orbit, AI cruise to another world, time warp
  • Borders, city search, news pins, live radio and weather clouds
  • Runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a laptop or phone with a recent GPU

PlanetView Live is an independent project. It is not affiliated with SpaceX or NASA. Earth imagery is from NASA.
