# Home Inventory for Insurance
Document what you own, room by room, so a claim after fire, flood or theft is fast and complete.

## How to do it in one afternoon
1. Walk each room with your phone recording video; narrate brand and rough age
2. Open closets and drawers
3. Photograph serial numbers and receipts for big items
4. Fill the table for anything over $100
5. Store the video and sheet in cloud storage (not just in the house)

## Don't forget
- Jewellery and watches (may need a separate rider)
- Bikes, sports and camping gear
- Tools
- Clothing (estimate per closet)
- Art, collectibles, instruments
- Electronics, chargers, games
- Kitchen small appliances and cookware

## Policy check
- [ ] Replacement cost or actual cash value?
- [ ] Limits on jewellery, electronics, cash, collectibles
- [ ] Sewer backup / overland flood coverage
- [ ] Deductible amount
- [ ] Additional living expenses coverage
