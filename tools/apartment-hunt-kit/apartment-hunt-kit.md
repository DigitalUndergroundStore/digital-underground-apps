# Apartment Hunt Kit (Canada)
Find, compare and secure a rental without getting burned.

## 1. Budget
- Rule of thumb: rent ≤ 30% of gross monthly income
- Add: hydro/electricity, heat (is it included?), internet, tenant insurance (~$15–35/mo), parking, laundry
- Move-in money: first month + last month's rent deposit (Ontario) or damage deposit (other provinces — rules vary), movers, setup fees

## 2. Must-haves vs nice-to-haves
| Must-have | Nice-to-have | Deal-breaker |
|---|---|---|

## 3. Viewing checklist
- [ ] Water pressure and hot water (run the shower)
- [ ] Signs of mould, leaks, pests (under sinks, corners, behind fridge)
- [ ] Windows open, lock, and seal; drafts
- [ ] Heating type and who controls it
- [ ] Phone signal in every room
- [ ] Outlets work; enough of them
- [ ] Laundry, storage, bike/locker
- [ ] Noise: visit once at night
- [ ] Smoke and CO alarms present
- [ ] Ask: why is the last tenant leaving?

## 4. Comparison sheet
| Address | Rent | Utilities | Commute | Size | Light | Laundry | Pets | Score /10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Application packet (have ready)
- Photo ID
- Proof of income (2 pay stubs or employment letter)
- Credit report (Equifax/TransUnion — free copies available)
- 2 references (landlord + work)
- Short intro note about you

## 6. Scam red flags
- Landlord "out of the country", can't show the unit
- Asks for deposit before viewing or signing
- Rent far below the area
- Wants e-transfer to hold the unit with no lease
- Pressure to decide today

## 7. Before you sign
- [ ] Read the lease (Ontario: Standard Lease form required for most rentals)
- [ ] Note what's included in writing
- [ ] Photograph every room on move-in day, timestamped
- [ ] Get tenant insurance
- [ ] Know your province's landlord-tenant board/tribunal
