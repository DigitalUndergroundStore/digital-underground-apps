Share a live location link that anyone can open

When you are on your way, Easy Tracker gives you a link to send. The other person opens it on their phone and sees you coming. They do not need an app or an account. The link stops when you tap Stop, or after 12 hours.

  • Free Android app (currently in testing)
  • Watchers need no app and no account, just a browser
  • The link expires automatically after 12 hours
  • Stop sharing any time with one tap

Frequently asked questions

Is Easy Tracker free?

Yes, it is free while in testing.

Does the other person need the app?

No. They open your link in any phone browser.

How long does the link last?

Until you tap Stop, or 12 hours at most.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Easy Tracker is Android only for now, but iPhone users can watch a link.
