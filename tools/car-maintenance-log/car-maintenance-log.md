## Seasonal checks
- **Fall:** winter tires on (below 7°C), battery test, wipers, washer fluid rated −40
- **Spring:** summer tires, alignment check after potholes, wash the undercarriage
- **Before a road trip:** tire pressure, oil level, lights, spare tire
