# 30-Day Declutter Challenge
One small area a day, 15–30 minutes each. By day 30 every room has had a pass.

## The rules
- Set a timer. Stop when it rings.
- Four boxes: **Keep, Donate, Recycle/Trash, Relocate**
- Donations leave the house within 48 hours
- If you haven't used it in a year and wouldn't buy it again, let it go
- Don't buy storage until the challenge is over

## Where things go
- Clothing and household items: local thrift stores or charity drop boxes
- Electronics: certified e-waste depots (many retailers take them back)
- Batteries and paint: municipal hazardous waste days
- Sellable items: list them in one batch on day 30, and donate whatever hasn't sold in 2 weeks

## Keep it clear
- One in, one out for clothes and kitchen items
- A 5-minute reset every evening
- A "maybe" box sealed and dated: if you didn't open it in 6 months, donate it unopened
