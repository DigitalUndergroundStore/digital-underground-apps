# New Parent Checklist
A calm, practical list for the weeks before and after baby arrives. Not medical advice — follow your care provider.

## 1. Third trimester to-dos
- [ ] Pack hospital/birth-centre bag (week 36)
- [ ] Install car seat; get it checked (many fire stations/clinics offer checks)
- [ ] Pre-register at the hospital if required
- [ ] Choose a family doctor or pediatrician
- [ ] Plan parental leave (Canada: apply for EI maternity/parental benefits; employer paperwork)
- [ ] Freeze 10–15 meals
- [ ] Line up help for the first 2 weeks

## 2. Hospital bag
**Parent:** ID & health card, phone charger (long cable), loose clothes, nursing bras, slippers, toiletries, pads, snacks, water bottle
**Baby:** 2–3 sleepers, hat, swaddle, going-home outfit, car seat
**Partner:** change of clothes, snacks, pillow

## 3. Nursery basics
- Safe sleep space: crib/bassinet meeting Canadian standards, firm mattress, fitted sheet only
- Diapers (newborn + size 1), wipes, cream
- Changing pad
- 6–8 sleepers, 4–6 bodysuits, swaddles
- Feeding: bottles/pump/formula or nursing supplies, burp cloths
- Baby thermometer, nail clippers, nasal aspirator

## 4. After birth paperwork (Canada)
- [ ] Register the birth with your province (usually within 30 days)
- [ ] Apply for provincial health card
- [ ] Apply for Social Insurance Number
- [ ] Apply for the Canada Child Benefit (often bundled with birth registration)
- [ ] Add baby to benefits/insurance
- [ ] Update will & beneficiaries
