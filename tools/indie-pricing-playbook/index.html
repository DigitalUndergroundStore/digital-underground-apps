Indie Pricing Playbook

Price your product with confidence: tiers, tests, copy and discount rules.

Part 1 — Find your price range (worksheet)

  1. What does the problem cost the customer per month (time × hourly value, or money lost)? CA$____
  2. What do they pay for the closest alternative? CA$____
  3. Target: 10–20% of value created, at or above the alternative if you're clearly better.
  4. Floor: (your monthly costs + desired income) ÷ realistic customer count in 12 months.
  5. Pick a number between floor and ceiling. Round to a "charm" price (9, 19, 29, 49).

Part 2 — Choose a model

Model Best for Watch out
One-time Templates, desktop tools, ebooks No recurring revenue; plan paid upgrades
Subscription Ongoing value, hosted services Must keep shipping; churn
Usage-based APIs, AI tools with real costs Bill shock; set caps
Freemium Wide top-of-funnel, viral tools Free tier too good = no upgrades
Lifetime deal Early cash, validation Long-term support cost; cap quantity

Part 3 — Tier builder (Good / Better / Best)

  • Starter — solves the core job for one person. Anchor price.
  • Pro (recommended) — everything a serious user needs. 2–3× Starter. Highlight it.
  • Team / Business — seats, admin, priority support. 3–5× Pro. Rules: tiers differ by value metric (seats, projects, volume), not by random feature gating. Max 3 public tiers. Annual = 2 months free.
