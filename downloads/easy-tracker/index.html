Easy Tracker

by Digital Underground · Android app

Free / In testing

Share your live location with one link. The person you send it to sees you coming on a map, with no app or account. Sharing ends when you tap Stop or after 12 hours.

Download for Android (APK)

Version 1.0.7 · 35.8 MB · Free / In testing: this is a test build, not in the app stores yet, so expect rough edges.

How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the notification or open easy-tracker-1.0.7.apk from your Downloads.
  3. If Android says installs from this source are blocked, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source (Install unknown apps) for your browser or Files app, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Allow location access when asked. To keep sharing while the phone is locked, choose Allow all the time.
  6. Your link is on the screen. Copy it and send it. Tap Stop when you arrive.

You can turn "Install unknown apps" back off afterwards. Updates install over the top of this version.

Privacy

Only your latest location is shared, no history is kept and nothing is sold. Read the privacy policy.
