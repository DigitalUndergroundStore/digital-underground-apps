Indie SaaS Ops Pack

The boring-but-critical systems for running a product solo.

1. Support macros (copy & adapt)

Got it / triage: "Thanks [Name] — got it. I'm looking into this now and will reply by [time]." Bug confirmed: "You're right, that's a bug. Fix is in progress; I'll email you as soon as it's live. Workaround for now: [steps]." Feature request: "Great idea — I've logged it. I can't promise a date, but I'll let you know if it ships." How-to: "Here's how: 1) … 2) … 3) …. Short video: [link]. Did that work?" Refund granted: "Done — refund issued; it can take 5–10 business days to appear. Sorry it wasn't a fit. Mind sharing what was missing?" Refund declined (policy): "Our policy covers [N] days; this purchase was [M] days ago. What I can do: [credit/extension/help]." Account deletion: "Your account and data have been deleted as of [date]. Receipts required for tax law are retained for [N] years." Angry customer: "I'm sorry — that's frustrating and it's on me. Here's what I'm doing: [action] by [time]." Closing the loop: "Following up: [issue] is fixed in [version]. Thanks for reporting it." Out of office: "I'm a solo maker and away until [date]. Urgent billing issues: [link]."

2. Incident communication

Status update template (post every 30–60 min until resolved):

[Investigating/Identified/Monitoring/Resolved] — [time, zone] What's affected: [feature]. Who: [all/some users]. What we're doing: [action]. Next update: [time]. Post-mortem (within 48h): summary · timeline · root cause · impact · what we changed · what we'll change.

3. Policies (outlines — have a professional review)

Refund policy: window (e.g. 14/30 days) · how to request · exceptions · processing time. Privacy policy outline: what you collect · why · processors (payments, analytics, email) · retention · user rights (access, deletion) · contact · cookies. Terms outline: licence/acceptable use · payment & renewals · cancellation · liability limit · termination · governing law (your province/state).
