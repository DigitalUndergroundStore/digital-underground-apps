About Digital Underground

Digital Underground is a free resource built to support independent makers. Listing is free and we take no commission, because payments never pass through us: buyers pay the maker directly through the maker’s own Stripe, Gumroad, App Store or website.

We also sell our own apps and books in the Digital Underground store, which is how the project pays its bills. Every listing comes from an independent creator. If we ever offer paid featured slots, they will be clearly labelled as sponsored.
