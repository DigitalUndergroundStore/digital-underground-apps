# Onboarding Email Kit
A short welcome sequence that gets users to their first win.

## Sequence
- Day 0 — Welcome + one first step
- Day 1 — The quickest win (with a GIF or screenshot)
- Day 3 — A tip most people miss
- Day 7 — Ask: how's it going? (reply-to you)
- Day 14 — Upgrade or share (one ask only)

## Templates
Day 0 subject: You're in — start here
Hi [name], thanks for trying [product]. Do this one thing first: [step]. Takes 2 minutes. — [you]

Day 1 subject: The 30-second win
Most people start by [action]. Here's how: [steps].

Day 3 subject: The hidden feature
[Feature] saves people [time]. Find it under [location].

Day 7 subject: Quick question
Is [product] doing what you hoped? Just hit reply — I read every one.

Day 14 subject: One small favour
If [product] helped, [upgrade link / share link] would mean a lot.

## Checklist
- [ ] Plain text, sent from a real person
- [ ] Unsubscribe link + mailing address (CASL / CAN-SPAM)
- [ ] One link per email
