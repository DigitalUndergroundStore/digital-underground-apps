# Apartment Hunt Kit (Canada)
Find, compare and secure a rental without getting burned.

## 1. Budget
- Rule of thumb: rent ≤ 30% of gross monthly income
- Add: hydro/electricity, heat (is it included?), internet, tenant insurance (~$15–35/mo), parking, laundry
- Move-in money: first month + last month's rent deposit (Ontario) or damage deposit (other provinces — rules vary), movers, setup fees
