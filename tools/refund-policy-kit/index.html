Refunds. If [product] isn't right for you, email [address] within [30] days of purchase for a full refund. No forms, no questions required (but we'd love to know why). Policy template (SaaS)

You can cancel anytime from Settings → Billing. You keep access until the end of your billing period. Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days. If you were charged by mistake, contact [address] and we'll fix it.
