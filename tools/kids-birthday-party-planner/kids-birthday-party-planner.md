# Kids' Birthday Party Planner
A 4-week plan, guest and RSVP tracker, budget, timeline for the day, and age-by-age activity ideas.

## 4 weeks out
- [ ] Theme and guest count (rule of thumb: age + 1 friends)
- [ ] Venue: home, park (book a shelter), or play place
- [ ] Date and 2-hour time slot (10–12 or 2–4 avoids naps and meals)
- [ ] Budget

## 2–3 weeks out
- [ ] Send invitations with an RSVP date and allergy question
- [ ] Order the cake or plan baking
- [ ] Plan 2–3 activities and a backup for rain

## Party-day timeline (2 hours)
| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | Arrivals, free play or colouring station |
| 0:20–0:50 | Main activity / game |
| 0:50–1:15 | Food |
| 1:15–1:35 | Cake + singing |
| 1:35–1:50 | Second game or presents (optional) |
| 1:50–2:00 | Favours, goodbyes |

## Ideas by age
- **1–3:** bubbles, music, sensory bin; keep it 90 minutes
- **4–6:** treasure hunt, freeze dance, decorate-your-own cupcake
- **7–9:** relay races, craft station, scavenger hunt, movie
- **10–12:** escape-room puzzle, pizza-making, sleepover-lite

## Day-before checklist
- [ ] Charge camera, candles + lighter, cake knife
- [ ] Garbage bags, wipes, first-aid kit
- [ ] Label allergy-safe food
- [ ] Favour bags ready by the door
