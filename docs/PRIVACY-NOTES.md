# Privacy / GDPR notes (internal)

**Not legal advice.** These pages were written to match how the site actually works as of 2026-10-18. Have a lawyer review /privacy/ and /terms/ before relying on them.

- No consent banner: no analytics/ad cookies, no tracking pixels, analytics are cookieless aggregate counts, rate-limit keys use hashed IPs, marketplace videos load only on click.
- If you ever add pixels (Meta, X, Google Ads), GA, or Google Fonts on DU pages, a consent banner is required first.
- Kerzy Labs games (e.g. /cyberarena/) load Google Fonts, cdnjs and Firebase; they are outside DU branding and were not changed. Consider self-hosting fonts there.
- Never publish the owner's personal Gmail on the site; use digitalunderground.store@proton.me.
