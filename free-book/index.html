What it's about

Crossing space was the easy part.

Adam Voss is twenty-four, grieving, and signed to a thirty-six-month contract building Artemis City, an unfinished lunar habitat where seals and air matter more than slogans. A breach alarm brings him face to face with Marcus Hale, a sharp Federal liaison who becomes Adam’s closest friend.

Then they leave for Mars. Marcus rises from advisor to general, Dr. Sarah Butler uncovers a lattice left by someone who came before humanity, and when Marcus declares independence, the war with Earth moves into orbit. Adam must choose between the friend who built a revolution and the people it is about to kill.

The transfer shuttle left Earthside dock at 04:12 UTC with a sound like a held breath finally released. Adam Voss sat in seat 14-C and watched the blue curve of the planet shrink through the cabin window. At twenty-four he had already learned how to look at beautiful things without letting them touch him.
