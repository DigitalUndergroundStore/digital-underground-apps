Oct 22: accounts are now synced to their email

Done and live. One email now means one account, whichever way someone signs in. Logging in with email, Google or X all open the same account with the same listings. Google already brings its verified email, so it joins the account that owns that email automatically. X doesn't share an email with us, so when someone signs in with X and their account has no email yet, they land on the Security page and are asked to add one. They get a confirm link by email, and once they open it (while still logged in), the email goes on their account. If that email already has an account, the two are joined into one, with all their listings, so there are no duplicate accounts. The Security page now has a "Sign-in methods" card showing Email, Google and X with a Link button for each, and the dashboard Profile tab shows a "Sign-in methods & 2FA" button plus a reminder to add an email when one is missing. Listings always use the account's email as their contact email, a signed-in creator's new listing uses their account email, and older accounts made from a listing email are tied to that email so Forgot password finds them. For safety, the confirm link only works in the browser of the account that asked for it, so a forwarded link can't join a stranger's X to your account. The old line on the log-in page about X getting its own account is replaced with "Your email, Google and X all open the same account once your email is on it."
