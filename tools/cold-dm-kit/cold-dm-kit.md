# Cold DM Kit
Find your first 50 users with honest, personal outreach.

## Rules
- [ ] Message people with the problem, not everyone
- [ ] Mention something specific about them
- [ ] One ask per message, easy to say no to
- [ ] Never more than 2 follow-ups

## Templates
1. Hey [name] — saw your post about [pain]. I built [product] for exactly that. Want a free copy for honest feedback?
2. Hi [name], quick one: how do you handle [task] today? Building something and want to get it right.
3. [Name], your [project] is great. I made a tool that [benefit] — happy to send it, no strings.
4. Follow-up (day 3): Bumping this in case it got buried — no worries if it's not a fit.
5. Follow-up (day 10): Last ping! If [pain] ever comes up, [link] is here.
6. Email subject: Quick question about [task]

## Tracking sheet columns
Name · Where found · Pain quoted · Sent date · Reply? · Follow-up 1 · Follow-up 2 · Outcome
