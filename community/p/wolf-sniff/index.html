About

Wolf Sniff is a browser extension for freelancers and agencies who quote website fixes.

One click on any site shows the CMS and version, page builder, WordPress theme (including child themes) and plugins, hosting, CDN and analytics. Every detection comes with the script, header or tag that gave it away, so you can check it yourself. A fix-difficulty score (0–100, with reasons) helps you price a job or walk away.

Free tier: platform and version, top 5 technologies with proof, and the WordPress fix-difficulty score; it runs in your browser.

Lifetime (€49 once, limited number of licences, then a yearly plan): every technology found (120+ known), full plugin list with versions, score breakdown, text/CSV export, local scan history, up to 3 browsers. 14-day refund by email.
