A weekly meal planner that writes your grocery list

Pantry Week plans your week of dinners (plus breakfast and lunch if you want them) from 1,000+ hand-checked recipes. It then builds one combined shopping list, sorted by aisle. It works offline on iPhone, Android and computers, includes meals from around the world, and is free during beta.

  • Plan 7 days in minutes: tap a meal, pick a recipe, or fill the week automatically
  • One shopping list, grouped by aisle, with quantities combined across recipes
  • Pantry tracking, so you do not buy what you already have
  • Cook mode shows one step at a time
  • Works offline and installs to your home screen. No account, no subscription

Frequently asked questions

Is Pantry Week a subscription?

No. Pantry Week is free while it is in beta. Get it on Gumroad for $0, install the web app, and it updates automatically. Please send feedback and photos of your meals. Early users who sign up with email in the app keep it free for life.

Does it work in Canada?

Yes. It is made in Ontario and priced in CA$. Recipes use common grocery-store ingredients, and the list adds grams next to pounds and ounces.

Does it need internet?

Only to unlock it the first time. After that it works offline on your phone or computer.

Can I add my own recipes?

Yes. You can add, edit and import your own recipes alongside the built-in ones.
