# User Interview Script Kit
Recruit 5–10 users, run 30-minute interviews that uncover real problems, and turn notes into decisions.

## Recruiting
> Hi [Name], I'm building [product] and trying to understand how people handle [problem]. Would you do a 20–30 min call this week? No sales pitch, just questions. Happy to send a [gift card / free month] as thanks.
Sources: current users, churned users, waitlist, communities where your audience hangs out.

## Rules
- Ask about the past, not hypotheticals ("last time you..." not "would you...")
- Listen 80%, talk 20%
- Never pitch during the interview
- Ask "why?" and "tell me more" a lot
- Record (with permission) or have a note-taker

## 30-minute script
1. **Warm-up (3 min):** role, context, typical week
2. **The problem (10 min):** "Tell me about the last time you [did task]." "What was hardest?" "What did you try?"
3. **Current solutions (7 min):** "What do you use today?" "What do you like / hate about it?" "What does it cost you in time or money?"
4. **Priorities (5 min):** "If you could fix one thing, what would it be?"
5. **Wrap (5 min):** "Anything I should have asked?" "Who else should I talk to?"

## For churned users
- "What were you hoping it would do?"
- "When did you decide to stop?"
- "What do you use now?"

## Notes template
| Participant | Role | Key quote | Pain | Current tool | Severity (1–5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Synthesis
- Tag every pain on a sticky note or row
- Group similar pains; count how many people mentioned each
- Top 3 by frequency × severity → next decisions
