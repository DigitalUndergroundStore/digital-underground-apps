# Recipe import — competitor export research (2026-10-11)

Sources are each app’s own help/docs. Pantry Week v3.4.0 does **not** claim a native Mealime file import.

## Mealime

- **Bulk export:** Not documented. Help covers **sharing** individual recipes (iOS) and **printing** a recipe or grocery list as PDF on the web — not a machine-readable export of your library.
  - [How to share recipes, grocery list & meal plans](https://support.mealime.com/article/101-how-to-share-recipes-grocery-list-meal-plans-mealime)
  - [How to print a recipe](https://support.mealime.com/article/90-how-to-print-a-recipe)
- **Third-party note:** Plan to Eat states Mealime has no bulk export; they suggest sharing a link and using a clipper one recipe at a time.
  - [Import recipes from Mealime](https://learn.plantoeat.com/help/import-recipes-from-mealime)

**Pantry Week path for Mealime switchers:** paste a **recipe link** (schema.org via `/api/recipe-fetch`) or **paste recipe text**.

## Paprika

- **Export formats:** HTML and **Paprika Recipe Format** (`.paprikarecipes` — ZIP of gzip-compressed JSON per recipe).
  - [What export formats do you support?](https://paprikaapp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051324613-What-export-formats-do-you-support)

## Plan to Eat

- **Export:** Recipe book as **CSV** (for importing elsewhere) and personal backup as **TXT**.
  - [Export your recipe book](https://learn.plantoeat.com/help/export-your-recipe-book)
  - [Account basics FAQ](https://learn.plantoeat.com/help/account-basics-faq)

## Mela

- **Formats:** `.melarecipe` (JSON) and `.melarecipes` (ZIP of `.melarecipe` files).
  - [File format](https://mela.recipes/fileformat/index.html)
  - [Import help](https://mela.recipes/help/)

## Samsung Food (formerly Whisk)

- Help emphasizes save/search/share and the clipper; **no documented bulk export format** for a full saved library in the articles reviewed.
  - [Getting started](https://support.samsungfood.com/hc/en-us/articles/18365507924372-Getting-Started-with-Samsung-Food-Save-Search-and-Share-Recipes)
  - [Share saved recipes](https://support.samsungfood.com/hc/en-us/articles/18588679568532-How-to-Share-Your-Recipes-with-Anyone)

## AnyList

- Recipes help covers import/share and opening recipe files; **no dedicated “export all recipes” help article** was found in the recipes topic index.
  - [Recipes help topics](https://help.anylist.com/topics/recipes/)
  - [Import Paprika into AnyList](https://help.anylist.com/articles/paprika-import/)
