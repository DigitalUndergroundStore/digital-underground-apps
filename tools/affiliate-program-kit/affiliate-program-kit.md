# Affiliate Program Starter Kit
Launch a simple affiliate program for a digital product or SaaS: terms, commission math, recruiting templates and a tracker.

## Decide the basics
| Decision | Common choice |
|---|---|
| Commission | 30–50% for digital products; 20–30% recurring for SaaS |
| Cookie window | 30–60 days |
| Payout | monthly, $50 minimum, after refund window |
| Tool | Gumroad affiliates, Lemon Squeezy, Rewardful, or a spreadsheet + codes |

## Commission math
Margin check: `price × commission% < price − fees − support cost`. On a $20 product at 40%, you keep ~$11 after fees.

## Simple terms (edit)
> Affiliates earn [X]% of net sales from their link for [N] days after a click. Payouts are monthly once the balance passes $[min], after the [14]-day refund window. No paid ads on our brand name, no coupon sites, no spam. We can end the agreement anytime; earned commissions are still paid.

## Who to recruit
- Happy customers (they already recommend you)
- Newsletter writers and YouTubers in your niche
- Makers of complementary tools
- Course creators who teach your audience

## Invite template
> Hi [Name], you mentioned [product] in [place]. Thank you! We just started an affiliate program: [X]% on every sale through your link. Here's your link and a swipe file: [link]. Want a free copy for a review?

## Affiliate swipe file (give them this)
- 3 tweet/post drafts
- 1 short newsletter blurb
- Cover images and screenshots
- The one-line pitch and who it's for
