Sinking Funds Planner

Stop irregular expenses from wrecking your budget. Save a little each month for the things you know are coming: car repairs, gifts, renewals, vacations.

How it works

  1. List every non-monthly expense for the next 12 months
  2. Divide each by the months until it's due
  3. Auto-transfer that total to a separate savings account on payday
  4. Spend from it guilt-free when the bill arrives
