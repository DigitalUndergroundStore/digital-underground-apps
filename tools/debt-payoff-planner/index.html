Even $50 a month extra makes a real difference over a few years.

3. Pick a method

Avalanche: pay minimums on everything, put all extra money on the highest interest rate first. Saves the most interest. Snowball: pay minimums on everything, put all extra money on the smallest balance first. Quick wins keep you motivated. Hybrid: clear one or two tiny balances first for momentum, then switch to avalanche.
