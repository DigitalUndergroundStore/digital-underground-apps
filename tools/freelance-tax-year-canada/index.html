Freelance Tax Year Checklist (Canada)

A month-by-month organizer for self-employed Canadians. General information, not tax advice — confirm with CRA guidance or an accountant.

1. Key dates at a glance

Date What
Mar 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Dec 15 Quarterly instalment dates (if CRA asks you to pay instalments)
~60 days after Dec 31 RRSP contribution deadline for the prior tax year
Apr 30 Balance owing due — even for self-employed
Jun 15 Filing deadline for self-employed individuals (and spouses)
GST/HST Annual filers: return due Jun 15 for individuals with business income; payment due Apr 30

2. Set up (do once)

  • [ ] Separate business bank account and card
  • [ ] Bookkeeping tool or spreadsheet with categories matching form T2125
  • [ ] Receipt capture habit (photo the same day)
  • [ ] Track revenue — once you pass $30,000 in taxable supplies over four consecutive quarters, you must register for GST/HST
  • [ ] Business number if registered
  • [ ] Mileage log if you use your car for work
  • [ ] Home office measurements (workspace sq ft ÷ home sq ft)

3. Monthly routine (30 minutes)

  • [ ] Reconcile bank and card
  • [ ] Categorise every expense
  • [ ] Chase unpaid invoices
  • [ ] Move 25–35% of profit to a tax savings account (adjust to your bracket)
  • [ ] File receipts in a dated folder
