# Student Semester Starter
Set up a semester so you're not cramming in week 12: calendar, study plan, budget and a residence packing list.

## 1. Week-zero setup
- [ ] Download every course syllabus
- [ ] Put every due date, test and exam in one calendar
- [ ] Note each course's grade breakdown
- [ ] Find office hours, TA emails and the late-work policy for each course
- [ ] Check the add/drop and withdrawal deadlines
- [ ] Buy or rent textbooks only after the first class (check the library and used copies first)

## 3. Weekly study plan
- 2–3 hours of study per hour of class is a common university rule of thumb
- Block study time like class time
- Review lecture notes within 24 hours (20 minutes)
- Sunday: 30-minute planning session for the week ahead

## 4. Big-assignment countdown
For each paper or project, work backward from the due date:
- 3 weeks out: pick a topic, check it with the prof
- 2 weeks out: research and outline
- 1 week out: full draft
- 3 days out: edit, cite, get feedback (writing centre)
- 1 day out: final read and submit

## 5. Exam prep
- [ ] Make a one-page summary per unit
- [ ] Do past exams under timed conditions
- [ ] Teach a topic out loud to a friend
- [ ] Sleep beats an all-nighter

## 7. Residence packing list
- Bedding (check bed size, often twin XL), pillow, towels
- Power bar with surge protector, extension cord (check residence rules)
- Laundry bag, detergent, quarters or laundry card
- Shower caddy and flip-flops
- Basic meds, first-aid kit
- Reusable water bottle, mug, plate, bowl, cutlery
- Headphones, laptop lock, chargers
- Copies of ID, health card and prescriptions

## 8. Well-being
- [ ] Save the campus counselling and crisis line numbers
- [ ] Find one club or group in the first 3 weeks
- [ ] Book any accessibility accommodations early
