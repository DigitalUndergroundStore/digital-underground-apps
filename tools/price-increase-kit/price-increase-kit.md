# Price Increase Kit
Raise your prices without losing trust: decide the new price, grandfather fairly, and announce it with ready templates.

## Should you raise?
- [ ] Conversion is healthy (above ~2–3% for digital products)
- [ ] You've added value since the last price
- [ ] Competitors charge more for similar
- [ ] Support/time costs have gone up
Three ticks = raise.

## How much
Test 20–40% steps. Track conversion for 2–4 weeks; if revenue per visitor goes up, keep it.

## Existing customers
- **Grandfather** current subscribers for 6–12 months or forever (most goodwill)
- Or give 30+ days notice and a lock-in offer (annual at the old rate)

## Timeline
- **T-14 days:** announce with the date and reason
- **T-3 days:** reminder: "last chance at the current price"
- **T-0:** switch prices, update pages, FAQs, screenshots

## Announcement template
> **Subject:** [Product] price changes on [date]
> Since launch, [product] has added [3 specific improvements]. On [date], the price goes from $[old] to $[new]. If you're already a customer, nothing changes for you [for N months / ever]. If you've been thinking about it, the current price holds until [date]: [link]
