# Press Kit Builder
Everything a journalist, podcaster or newsletter writer needs to cover your product in five minutes.

## 1. What goes in a press kit
- [ ] One-line description (under 15 words)
- [ ] 100-word and 300-word descriptions
- [ ] Founder bio (50 words) and headshot (square, 1000px+)
- [ ] Logo: SVG, PNG on light, PNG on dark
- [ ] 3–6 screenshots (1600px wide, no browser clutter)
- [ ] 30–60s demo video or GIF
- [ ] Key facts: launch date, pricing, platforms, location, team size
- [ ] 2–3 customer quotes with name and role
- [ ] Press contact email that a real person reads
- [ ] One zip with all of it, linked from /press

## 2. Description formulas
**One-liner:** [Product] helps [who] [do outcome] without [pain].
**100 words:** problem (1 sentence) → what it does (2) → who it's for (1) → what's different (1) → price/availability (1).
**300 words:** add origin story, a number (users, time saved), and a customer quote.

## 3. Founder bio template
> [Name] is a [role] based in [city]. Before [Product], they [credible past thing]. They built [Product] after [specific frustration]. [Name] writes about [topic] at [link].

## 5. Pitch email (short)
Subject: [Product] — [the angle in 6 words]

Hi [Name], I read your piece on [specific article]. I built [Product], which [one-liner]. The angle for your readers: [why now / surprising number]. Press kit with screenshots and quotes: [link]. Happy to give you early access or answer anything. — [Name]
