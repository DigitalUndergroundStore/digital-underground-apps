Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to list?

No. Listing is free.

What can I list?

Indie apps, games, developer tools, browser projects and open-source software.

How do AI agents find my listing?

Through our public listings API, openapi.json and the /mcp server, which assistants can query.

Does a listing help my SEO?

Yes. Every listing gets its own indexed page in our sitemap with a followed link back to your site.

Are listings reviewed?

Yes. Every submission is screened automatically. Clean listings go live right away; anything flagged waits for a manual review.
