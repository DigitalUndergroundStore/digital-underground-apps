Privacy and email policy

Last updated October 17, 2026.

Who we are

Digital Underground is an independent creator business based in Ontario, Canada. It sells apps, guides and AI tools at digitalundergroundstore.com and on Gumroad. Contact us through the support page.

Email list (Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, CASL)

  • We only add you to our list when you give express consent. You do that by typing your email and ticking an unticked box that says you agree to get emails from Digital Underground. The box is never pre-ticked.
  • We record your email address, the date and time you agreed, the page you signed up on, and the exact consent wording you agreed to. This is our proof of consent.
  • We send new apps, product updates and occasional offers. We aim for a few emails a month at most.
  • Every email identifies Digital Underground as the sender, includes contact details, and has a working unsubscribe link. We act on unsubscribe requests right away, and always within 10 business days, as CASL requires. You can also unsubscribe by replying "unsubscribe" or through the support page.
  • You can still download the free gift after you unsubscribe.

How we store and use your data

Subscriber records are stored in Cloudflare's key-value storage. We never sell, rent or share your email address. We use it only to send Digital Underground emails. Purchases are processed by Gumroad, which has its own privacy policy. We do not use tracking pixels for advertising on this site.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time through the support page. If you are in Canada, PIPEDA gives you these rights. Other regions, such as the EU and UK under GDPR, offer similar rights, and we honour them too.
