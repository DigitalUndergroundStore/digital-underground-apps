Home Maintenance Calendar

A season-by-season checklist that keeps small fixes from turning into big bills. Written for Canadian winters.

Fall (Sept–Nov)

  • [ ] Clean gutters and downspouts after leaves drop
  • [ ] Shut off and drain outdoor taps; disconnect hoses
  • [ ] Furnace service + replace filter
  • [ ] Test smoke and CO alarms; replace batteries
  • [ ] Seal drafts: weatherstrip doors, caulk windows
  • [ ] Reverse ceiling fans to clockwise (pushes warm air down)
  • [ ] Bring in patio furniture; store BBQ propane outside, upright
  • [ ] Winter tires on (by mid-Nov in most provinces)
  • [ ] Stock salt/ice melt, shovel, car snow brush

Winter (Dec–Feb)

  • [ ] Furnace filter every 1–3 months
  • [ ] Keep vents and dryer exhaust clear of snow
  • [ ] Watch for ice dams; keep downspouts clear
  • [ ] Check pipes in exterior walls on very cold nights (open cabinet doors)
  • [ ] Run humidifier 30–40% indoors; watch for window condensation
  • [ ] Test sump pump before spring melt

Spring (Mar–May)

  • [ ] Inspect roof for missing shingles after winter
  • [ ] Clean gutters again
  • [ ] Check grading — soil should slope away from the foundation
  • [ ] Turn outdoor taps back on; check for leaks
  • [ ] Service AC / heat pump; clean outdoor unit
  • [ ] Window screens on; wash windows
  • [ ] Summer tires on (after the last frost)

Summer (Jun–Aug)

  • [ ] Clean dryer vent duct end to end
  • [ ] Inspect deck/fence; reseal wood every 2–3 years
  • [ ] Trim trees and shrubs away from the house
  • [ ] Check caulking in bathroom and kitchen
  • [ ] Flush water heater (yearly)

Monthly quick checks (10 minutes)

  • [ ] Look under sinks for leaks
  • [ ] Clean range hood filter
  • [ ] Run water in unused drains (keeps traps full)
  • [ ] Test GFCI outlets (press TEST, then RESET)
