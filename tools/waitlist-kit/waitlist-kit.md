# Waitlist & Pre-Launch Kit
Build a list of people who want your product before launch day.

## Landing page copy (fill the brackets)
- **Headline:** [Outcome] without [pain]. — e.g. "Invoices sent in 60 seconds, without spreadsheets."
- **Sub-headline:** [Product] is a [category] for [who] that [key mechanism].
- **3 bullets:** [benefit + proof] / [benefit + proof] / [benefit + proof]
- **CTA button:** "Get early access" (not "Submit")
- **Under the button:** "Launching [month]. One email at launch, no spam."
- **Social proof slot:** "[N] makers already on the list" — show it once N ≥ 50.

## Waitlist emails
**1. Confirmation (instant)** — Subject: You're on the list for [Product]
> Thanks for joining. You're #[N]. I'm building [Product] because [one-line story]. Hit reply and tell me the one thing you'd want it to do — I read every answer.

**2. Build update (weekly)** — Subject: [Product] update: [specific thing you shipped]
> This week: [shipped thing + screenshot]. Next: [next thing]. Question for you: [one question].

**3. Early access (T-3 days)** — Subject: Your early access to [Product]
> You're in before the public. Link: [URL]. Early-list price: [price/discount] until [date].

**4. Launch day** — Subject: [Product] is live
> It's live: [URL]. If it helps, the best support is [upvote / share / reply with feedback].

## 14-day pre-launch calendar
| Day | Do |
|---|---|
| -14 | Landing page live, waitlist form tested end-to-end |
| -13 | Post the "why I'm building this" story on X/LinkedIn |
| -12 | DM 20 people who've complained about the problem |
| -11 | First build-update email |
| -10 | Share a 20-sec demo clip |
| -9 | Ask 5 list members for a 15-min call |
| -8 | Prepare launch assets (screens, GIF, copy) |
| -7 | Second build-update email + pricing teaser |
| -6 | Line up 3 friends to share on launch day |
| -5 | Write the launch post + FAQ |
| -4 | Final QA: signup, payment, emails, mobile |
| -3 | Early-access email |
| -2 | Collect first testimonials from early users |
| -1 | Schedule posts, sleep |
| 0 | Launch (use the free Launch Day Kit) |
