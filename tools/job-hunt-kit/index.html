Job Hunt Kit

A simple system to track applications, write sharper cover letters and prep for interviews.

1. Application tracker (CSV columns)

company,role,link,date_applied,source,contact,status(applied/screen/interview/offer/rejected),next_step,next_date,salary_range,notes Goal: 5 tailored applications a week beats 50 generic ones.

2. Resume bullet formula

[Action verb] + [what] + [result with a number] - "Cut invoice processing time 40% by automating approvals in [tool]." - "Grew newsletter from 800 to 5,200 subscribers in 9 months." Tailor: mirror 3–5 key words from the job posting in your top bullets.

3. Cover letter template (≤250 words)

Hi [Hiring manager name],

I'm applying for [Role] because [specific reason about this company — product, mission, recent news].

In my current/last role at [Company], I [biggest relevant result with a number]. I also [second relevant result]. That's the kind of work your posting describes in [specific responsibility].

What I'd bring in the first 90 days: [1–2 concrete ideas].

I'd love to talk. Thanks for reading, [Name] · [phone] · [LinkedIn/portfolio]

4. Networking message

Hi [Name], I'm exploring [role type] roles and really admire [their team/work]. Could I ask you 3 quick questions about what it's like at [Company]? 15 minutes, whenever suits you.

5. Interview prep — STAR stories

Prepare 6 stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for: a big win · a conflict · a failure & what you learned · leading without authority · a tight deadline · a time you changed someone's mind.

6. Common questions to rehearse

  1. Tell me about yourself (60–90 sec: present → past → why this role)
  2. Why this company?
  3. Why are you leaving?
  4. Biggest strength / weakness
  5. Salary expectations ("Based on my research, CA$X–Y; flexible on total package")

7. Questions to ask them

  • What does success look like in the first 6 months?
  • What's the biggest challenge for the team right now?
  • How do you measure performance here?
  • What's the next step in the process?

8. Thank-you note (send within 24h)

Thanks for your time today, [Name]. I enjoyed hearing about [specific topic]. It reinforced my interest in [Role] — especially [thing]. Happy to share anything else that helps.

9. Offer checklist

  • [ ] Base salary, bonus, equity
  • [ ] Benefits (health, dental, RRSP/pension match)
  • [ ] Vacation days, remote/hybrid policy
  • [ ] Start date, probation period
  • [ ] Get it in writing before resigning
