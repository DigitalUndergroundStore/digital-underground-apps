# Road Trip Kit
Car prep, packing and planning for a smooth drive — summer or winter.

## 1. One week before
- [ ] Oil, coolant, washer fluid topped up
- [ ] Tire pressure and tread (winter: proper winter tires)
- [ ] Wipers, lights, brakes checked
- [ ] Roadside assistance card in glovebox
- [ ] Offline maps downloaded
- [ ] Route with fuel/EV charging stops every 2–3 hours

## 2. Car kit (keep in trunk)
- Jumper cables or booster pack
- First-aid kit
- Flashlight + spare batteries
- Blanket, gloves, toque
- Ice scraper and snow brush (winter)
- Small shovel, traction mats or kitty litter (winter)
- Water, non-perishable snacks
- Phone charger + power bank
- Paper towels, garbage bags, wet wipes
- Tire gauge

## 3. Packing list
**Clothes:** layers, rain jacket, comfortable shoes
**Toiletries:** small kit, sunscreen, meds
**Docs:** licence, registration, insurance, passport (if crossing border), reservations
**Fun:** playlists & podcasts downloaded, car games, travel pillow

## 4. Kids & pets add-on
- Snack box per kid, tablet + headphones, sick bag
- Pet: leash, water bowl, food, waste bags, vaccination records
