Dunning & Churn Email Pack

Recover failed payments and save cancelling customers. Ready-to-paste emails, timing, in-app copy and a recovery tracker for subscription products.

1. Why this matters

Failed payments (expired cards, insufficient funds, bank blocks) are often a big part of subscription churn. Most of these customers didn't mean to leave. A short, friendly sequence brings many of them back.

2. Settings to check first

  • [ ] Turn on your payment processor's automatic retries (Stripe "Smart Retries" or similar)
  • [ ] Turn on automatic card updates where your processor supports it
  • [ ] Send a "card expiring soon" email 30 and 7 days before expiry
  • [ ] Have a hosted page where a customer can update their card without logging in

3. Failed payment sequence

Email 1 — Day 0 (immediately) Subject: Your payment for [Product] didn't go through

Hi [first name], We tried to charge your card ending in [last4] for [Product] and it didn't go through. That happens; usually it's an expired card or a bank check. Update your card in 30 seconds: [Update payment button] Your account stays active while you sort it out. — [Name], [Product]

Email 2 — Day 3 Subject: Quick reminder: update your card for [Product]

Hi [first name], just a nudge. We'll try your card again on [date]. If you'd rather use a different card, here's the link: [Update payment]. Anything we can help with? Just reply.

Email 3 — Day 7 Subject: Your [Product] account will pause on [date]

Hi [first name], we still couldn't process your payment. On [date] your account will pause and [specific thing they'd lose: projects, saved data, team access] will be on hold. [Update payment] If [Product] isn't right for you anymore, no hard feelings. Reply and tell us why; it really helps.

Email 4 — Day 14 (final) Subject: Your [Product] account is paused

Hi [first name], your account is now paused. Your data is safe for [X days]. Reactivate any time: [Update payment]
