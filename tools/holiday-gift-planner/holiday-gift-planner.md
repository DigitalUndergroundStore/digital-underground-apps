## Step 4 — Gift idea prompts by person type
- **Hard to shop for:** an experience (class, show, dinner), a subscription, a "consumable upgrade" (better coffee, nice olive oil)
- **Kids:** one want, one need, one wear, one read
- **Coworkers:** under CA$20 — good chocolate, a plant, a notebook
- **Hosts:** something for after the party (breakfast basket, candles)
- **Far away:** order direct-to-them early; send an e-card the day it lands

## Money-saving rules
1. Decide the list before you shop sales.
2. Use a price tracker for big items; buy only if it hits your target.
3. Keep receipts in one folder (gift receipts too).
4. Pay with one card so you can see the running total.
5. Once the gift column hits budget, stop. The buffer is for emergencies.
