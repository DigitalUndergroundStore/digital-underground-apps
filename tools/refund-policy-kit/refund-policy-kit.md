# Refund & Returns Policy Kit
Fair, clear refund policies for digital products, SaaS and small physical shops, plus reply templates. Not legal advice: check consumer law where you sell (e.g. EU 14-day withdrawal rules, Quebec, card-network rules).

## Pick your policy
| Business | Common policy | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Digital download | 14- or 30-day no-questions refund | Generous policies cut chargebacks and lift sales |
| SaaS monthly | Cancel anytime, no partial-month refund | Refund first charge if they never used it |
| SaaS annual | Prorated or full refund within 30 days | |
| Physical goods | 30 days, unused, original packaging | Say who pays return shipping |
| Services | Deposit non-refundable after kickoff | Define milestones |

## Policy template (digital)
> **Refunds.** If [product] isn't right for you, email [address] within [30] days of purchase for a full refund. No forms, no questions required (but we'd love to know why). Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. We may decline refunds for repeated purchase-and-refund patterns.

## Policy template (SaaS)
> You can cancel anytime from Settings → Billing. You keep access until the end of your billing period. Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days. If you were charged by mistake, contact [address] and we'll fix it.

## Policy template (physical)
> Return unused items in original packaging within 30 days for a refund to your original payment. [We cover / You cover] return shipping. Final-sale items are marked. Damaged on arrival? Send a photo within 7 days and we'll replace it free.

## Where to show it
- [ ] Footer link on every page
- [ ] One line near the buy button ("30-day refund")
- [ ] Order confirmation email
- [ ] FAQ page

## Reply templates
**Approved:** "Done: your refund of [amount] is on its way and should land in 5–10 business days. If you have a sec, what didn't work for you? It helps us improve."
**Outside window:** "Your purchase was [X] days ago, past our [30]-day window, so I can't refund it, but here's [fix/credit/alternative]."
**Possible chargeback:** respond fast, refund if reasonable; a chargeback costs you the fee plus the sale.
