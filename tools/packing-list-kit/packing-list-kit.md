# Packing Lists by Trip Type
Tick-off lists for a weekend away, a beach week, a city break, camping, business travel and travel with kids, plus a base list every trip shares.

## Base list (every trip)
- [ ] ID / passport, wallet, cards, travel insurance details
- [ ] Phone, charger, power bank, adapter if abroad
- [ ] Medications + copies of prescriptions
- [ ] Toiletries (3-1-1 bag for carry-on liquids)
- [ ] Underwear and socks (days + 1)
- [ ] Sleepwear, a layer, comfortable shoes
- [ ] Reusable water bottle, snacks
- [ ] Booking confirmations saved to your phone

## Weekend away (2–3 nights)
- [ ] 2 outfits + 1 nicer option
- [ ] One pair of extra shoes max
- [ ] Book or e-reader
Fits in a carry-on or backpack.

## Beach week
- [ ] Swimsuits ×2, cover-up, sun hat, sunglasses
- [ ] Sunscreen (reef-safe), after-sun, bug spray
- [ ] Sandals, beach towel, dry bag
- [ ] Waterproof phone pouch

## City break
- [ ] Walking shoes broken in
- [ ] Small crossbody or anti-theft bag
- [ ] Compact umbrella
- [ ] One outfit for a nice dinner

## Camping
- [ ] Tent, sleeping bag rated for the low temperature, pad, pillow
- [ ] Headlamp + spare batteries
- [ ] Stove, fuel, lighter, pot, utensils, biodegradable soap
- [ ] Water filter, cooler, food plan
- [ ] First-aid kit, knife/multitool, rope
- [ ] Rain jacket, warm layers, extra socks

## Business trip
- [ ] Laptop, charger, presentation backup on a USB key
- [ ] 2–3 wrinkle-resistant outfits, one blazer
- [ ] Business cards, notebook
- [ ] Gym clothes (optional)

## With kids
- [ ] Snacks, wipes, change of clothes in the carry-on
- [ ] Tablet + headphones, downloaded shows
- [ ] Comfort toy, nightlight
- [ ] Car seat / travel stroller
- [ ] Kids' meds (fever reducer, allergy)

## Before leaving home
- [ ] Mail held, plants watered, trash out
- [ ] Thermostat down, appliances off
- [ ] Spare key with a neighbour
- [ ] Check-in done, boarding pass on phone
