# Black Friday Sale Kit for Makers
Plan a Black Friday / Cyber Monday sale for a digital product or SaaS: offer, timeline, emails, social posts and a results tracker.

## Pick the offer
| Offer | Good for |
|---|---|
| 30–50% off | digital products, templates |
| Annual plan discount | SaaS (locks in revenue) |
| Bundle price | multiple products |
| Bonus item | if you don't discount |
| Lifetime deal (careful) | only with clear limits |
Decide: discount, start/end (e.g. Fri 00:00 – Mon 23:59), code or automatic, and whether existing customers get it.

## Timeline
- **2 weeks out:** set up codes, sale banner, test checkout
- **1 week out:** "early access" list sign-up post
- **Thursday:** early access email
- **Friday:** launch email + posts
- **Sunday:** reminder
- **Monday (Cyber Monday):** "last day" email morning + "final hours" evening
- **Tuesday:** take down banners and codes

## Email templates
> **Launch:** Subject: [Product] is [X]% off until Monday. Body: The biggest sale of the year: [offer] on [product], no code needed. It helps you [benefit]. Ends Monday at midnight. [link]
> **Reminder:** Subject: Halfway through the sale. Body: Quick reminder that [offer] ends Monday. Here's what people are using it for: [use case].
> **Last call:** Subject: Final hours: [X]% off ends tonight.

## Social posts
- Friday: "Black Friday: [product] [X]% off till Monday. [one-line benefit] [link]"
- Sunday: a customer result or screenshot + the offer
- Monday: "Last day" + countdown
