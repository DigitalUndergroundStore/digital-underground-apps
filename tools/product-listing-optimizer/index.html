# Product Listing Optimizer
A checklist and scorecard to rewrite any digital-product or marketplace listing (Gumroad, Etsy, app stores, your own store) so it converts.

## Title formula
[Outcome] + [Product type] + [For whom]. Example: "Launch-Week Email Sequence — 7 ready emails for indie makers". Keep the key phrase in the first 40 characters.

## Description skeleton
1. Hook: the problem in their words
2. What it is in one sentence
3. What's inside (bullets with numbers: "7 emails, 1 tracker")
4. Who it's for / who it's not for
5. Preview link

7. CTA

## Before/after example
